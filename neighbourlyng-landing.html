Lagos has 15 million residents. Zero reliable, public data on what it's actually like to live there.

  • ⚡
    Power supply is invisible. Residents discover NEPA schedules only after signing their lease.
  • 🌊
    Flooding devastates block-by-block. Adjacent streets face radically different rainy seasons.
  • 🏠
    Estate agents have no incentive to share bad news. Word-of-mouth only travels so far.
  • 📡
    Network strength varies per street. Critical for remote workers choosing where to live.

Process

How NeighbourlyNG
works.

Step one
Residents submit structured ratings
A mobile-first, multi-step form walks you through each category. One review per neighbourhood per 90 days prevents spam and keeps data fresh.

Step two
Scores aggregate into heatmaps
MongoDB geospatial queries compute averages across bounding boxes. Leaflet.js renders choropleth heatmaps filterable by category and score threshold.
Step three
Renters make informed decisions
Neighborhood profile pages show full scorecards, review history, and trend over time — enabling data-driven decisions, for the first time, without estate agents.

Why it works

Built for trust.
Designed to scale.

90d
Review Cooldown
One review per user per neighbourhood per 90 days. Rate limiting and JWT auth prevent coordinated abuse while keeping data fresh.
2d↗
Geospatial Index
MongoDB 2dsphere indexes on coordinates enable millisecond bounding-box queries. GeoJSON polygon storage for LGA boundaries.
0₦
Free Forever
NeighbourlyNG is open-access and free for all renters. OSM tile layers, open-source stack. No paywalls, no estate agent partnerships.
